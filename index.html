Flagged accounts

The scan runs every night at 05:10 UTC over the last 7 days, and on demand. An account is flagged when any rule trips: daily, 1,500 or more reps in one day, not counting jumping jacks, high knees or the plank; jacks or knees, 3,000 or more jumping jacks, or 3,000 or more high knees, in one day; nonstop, 400 or more reps with no pause over 10 seconds; steady, a session of 150 or more reps whose pace barely changes (the middle 80% of rep times within 22% of the typical rep); exact, 100 or more reps whose exact rep-to-rep times vary by less than 6% (new app builds send every rep's time). While an account is flagged, watched or confirmed, a share of its camera sessions records one minute of body-position points (no video) for replay here. The player sees nothing.
